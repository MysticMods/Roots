Roots
=====

Roots 1 was originally authored by Elucent. Roots 2 was authored by Elucent, Alexismachina, ShadowGamerXY, werty1125 and others.

Roots 3 was authored by EpicSquid, Davoleo, Noobanidus, Martacus, with assets by Vulpie, AranaiRa, Androsa, Kalaxi, Raddish, 5B, GentlemanGnu. Audio assets were created by Lich, ScrewyMacGyver and KitzahTheNarrator.

Roots 4 was authored by Noobanidus, with assets by Ridanisaurus, Vulpie and Noobanidus.

The "Alertness" warning sound was created by DatBloxyBirb and released as CC0 (Public Domain) on [freesound.org](https://freesound.org/people/DatBloxyBirb/sounds/739749/).

AI Declaration
==============

No generative AI or generative AI-derived art assets were used in this mod.

GitHub CoPilot was used for auto-completion and generation of small repetitive segments, such as `ModItems`.

ChatGPT was consulted for working out the vector mathematics used in particles and rendering.
