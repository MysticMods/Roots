## Blooming

This ritual randomly generates and plants flowers based on the [`roots:ritual/blooming/eligible_flowers`](https://github.com/MysticMods/Roots/blob/1.21.1/src/generated/resources/data/roots/tags/block/ritual/blooming/eligible_flowers.json) tag.

By placing ritual pedestals nearby, any item on them that is also contained within the [`roots:ritual/blooming/eligible_pedestal_flowers`](https://github.com/MysticMods/Roots/blob/1.21.1/src/generated/resources/data/roots/tags/block/ritual/blooming/eligible_pedestal_flowers.json) will be generated instead.

Status: functional but missing visuals.
