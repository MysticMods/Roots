# Fairy

The denizens of the fairy grove favour the herb pereskia and orderly collections of flowers. They appreciated trades made with villagers and piglins.

# Sprouting

The denizens of the sprouting grove appreciate the herb wildewheet, the growth and harvest of crops. They favour unique crops and the growth of trees and plants.

# Wild

The wild groven is fond of spiritleaf.
