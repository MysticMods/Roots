## Renewing Resources

[<Asset location="roots:wildroot"/>Wildroot](resources/crops/wildroot) can be planted as a crop in any tilled soil.

The [<Asset location="roots:ritual_overgrowth"/>Overgrowth](mechanics/rituals/overgrowth) ritual causes <Asset location="roots:creeping_grove_moss"/> [Creeping Grove Moss](resources/grove_moss) to spawn which, when broken, will yield <Asset location="roots:grove_moss"/>[Grove Moss](resources/grove_moss).

You can rarely find Wildroot and [<Asset location="roots:grove_spores"/>Grove Spores](resources/grove_spores) when breaking grass and ferns.

Grove Spores can be used by right-clicking on a block that has a water source adjacent to it. Doing so will grow Creeping Grove Moss.

Creeping Grove Moss grown from grove spores has a chance to drop further Grove Spores. Creeping Grove Moss grown via the Overgrowth ritual will never drop Grove Spores.

See the [<Asset location="roots:ritual_overgrowth"/>Overgrowth](mechanics/rituals/overgrowth) ritual entry for more details on automating grove moss production.

## Wooden Shears

Crafting <Asset location="roots:wooden_shears"/>[Wooden Shears](tools/wooden_shears) allows you to access wool and leaf blocks, etc, sooner than you otherwise would.

## Foraging

Using a <Asset location="roots:knives"/>[Knife](tools/knives) to break leaves and grasses (and other blocks contained within the `roots:foragable/single` and `roots:foragable/double`) has an increased chance to drop wild roots, grove moss and grove spores. This attribute is called "foraging".

The following tiers have the following levels of foraging. Each level of foraging increases the chances of finding wild roots, grove moss and grove spores.

- <Asset location="roots:wooden_knife"/>Wood: level 1
- <Asset location="roots:stone_knife"/>Stone: level 2
- <Asset location="roots:iron_knife"/>Iron or <Asset location="roots:copper_knife"/>Copper: level 3
- <Asset location="roots:golden_knife"/>Gold or <Asset location="roots:silver_knife"/>Silver: level 4
- <Asset location="roots:diamond_knife"/>Diamond: level 5
- <Asset location="roots:netherite_knife"/>Netherite: level 6

Foraging can also be increased with the Foraging enchantment, but that is not yet implemented.

## Bark and Runed Logs

Knives are also used to strip <Asset location="roots:barks"/>[Bark](resources/bark) from logs. If you hold wild root or grove moss in your off-hand, you can instead convert logs into <Asset location="roots:runed_logs"/>[Runed Logs]().

## Runestone

The next major step is creating a source of <Asset location="roots:runestone"/> [Runestone](resources/runestone).

The default recipe allows you to convert 8 blocks of any type of stone into 3 runestone.

Runestone can then be used to craft a <Asset location="roots:pyre"/> [Pyre](mechanics/rituals/pyres) (see the rituals section), a <Asset location="roots:mortar"/>[Mortar](mechanics/mortar-and-pestle), a <Asset location="roots:primal_grove_stone"/>[Primal Grove Stone]() and a <Asset location="roots:grove_crafter"/> [Grove Crafter]().

## Grove Stone

Use Just Enough Items to see the recipe for a grove stone.

Once placed, you will need to use the <Asset location="roots:ritual_grove_supplication"/>[Grove Supplication]() ritual to activate the Grove Stone.

## Grove Crafter

A grove crafter requires a nearby, activated Grove Stone. If it does not have one, the grove display will be dull and it will display an icon above it.

Once you have an Activated Grove Stone, you can use a Grove Crafter to easily create Runestone.

Grove crafting recipes require you to place the relevant ingredients on Pedestals.

<Asset location="roots:grove_pedestal"/>[Grove Pedestals]() can contain one item and can be filled by a hopper or other similar methods.

<Asset location="roots:wildwood_pedestal"/>[Wildwood Pedestals]() can contain a full stack of an item.

Empty and <Asset location="roots:display_pedestal"/>[Display Pedestals]() do not contribute to a recipe.

Once a valid recipe is found, the resulting item will display above the grove crafter.

Simply right-click the grove crafter with a knife or an empty hand to begin the craft.

The grove crafter will attempt to place the results of crafts into adjacent containers. If none are found, the items will be spawned in the world.

Please see Just Enough Items for relevant grove crafting recipes and their requirements.

## Mortar & Pestle

Please see Just Enough Items for the recipes for crafting a mortar and pestle.

Items can be placed into the mortar simply by right-clicking the item on the mortar. A single item is placed at a time, and the mortar can contain up to 5 items.

Items can be removed by right-clicking the mortar with an empty hand.

Once the mortar is full with a valid recipe, simply right click the mortar with a pestle until it finishes crafting. The number of times required to craft with the mortar varies with recipes.

<Callout variant='warning'>
Just Enough Items currently does not display the number of times required to activate a recipe
</Callout>

## Runic Shears

Please see Just Enough Items for the runic shears recipe.

Runic shears can be used on specific blocks to convert them into other blocks. By default, this is the acquisition source for wildewheet seeds and spiritleaf seeds. These recipes have a considerable durability cost.

Please see Just Enough Items for these block interactions.

Runic shears can also be used on entities. By default, this is the acquisition source for fey leather. Using runic shears on an entity triggers a cooldown, during which you cannot use runic shears on that same entity.

Please see Just Enough Items for these entity interactions.

EMI currently does not display these recipes correctly, please use JEI
</Callout>
