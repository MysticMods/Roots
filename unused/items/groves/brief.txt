# Requirements:

16x16 pixel coloured embellishments of:

elemental/elemental_grove_generic.png
fairy/fairy_grove.png
fungal/fungal_grove_baffle_cap.png
primal/primal_option_1_grove_moss.png (OR wildroot)
sprouting/sprouting_grove.png
twilight/twilight_grove.png
wild/wild_grove.png

As it stands, the icons are excellent for use in items as overlays (see reference folder) but insufficient for use as UI elements.

These icons will be used as follows:

- Grove Power: As an ingredient in JEI recipes to denote the amount of "grove power" generated by or consumed by a recipe. 
- Grove Reputation: As an ingredient in JEI recipes to denote the amount of reputation that will be gained (or lost) by performing a specific action
- Associated UI: In various UI elements, most specifically the Reputation UI (planned but not designed)

For each folder are included:

- The herb associated with that grove (or herbs, if there are multiple)
- The seed of that herb (if any)
- The grove stone texture (which is animated) associated with that grove
- Any crop blocks associated with that herb (if any)
- Any other relevant blocks associated with that herb (if any)

The fungal grove contains both the block and item forms of the bafflecap mushroom.

The elemental grove contains all of the elemental herbs but has a separate icon to denote the elemental groves in general. It also contains the various elemental soils.

The sprouting grove also contains all of the sprout textures. 

# Summary of Groves

# Elemental

The elemental grove is linked to the various elemental powers. Further ranks in the elemental grove will allow for the summoning and control of elementals, specifically in machines that allow for automation. Progression to the ultimate tier of the elemental grove reputation requires you to defeat one or more boss-level elementals.

Reputation is gained by growing elemental crops, learning elemental spells, starting elemental rituals, breaking ore blocks, killing withers, and entering the nether for the first time.

Primary herbs are stalicripe, inferno bulb, dewgonia and cloud berry.

# Fairy

The fairy grove is linked to ordered trade and beauty. Progression allows you to create fairy huts, which allow you to trade.

Reputation is gained by learning Fairy-adjacent spells, starting Fairy-adjacent rituals, trading with villagers, trading with piglins, trading with fairy huts, curing villagers from zombie curse, and lost by killing traders (villagers or piglins).

Primary herb is pereskia.

# Fungal

The fungal grove is linked to natural decay and rallying against undeath.

Primary herb is bafflecap.

# Primal

The primal grove is the "overworld" grove. Reputation cannot be gained and lost with the primal grove.

The associated herbs are grove moss and wildroots.

# Sprouting

The sprouting grove is linked to the growth of crops. Progression both allows for the summoning of various helpful sprouts, as well as requiring you to complete challenges involving larger sprout NPCs.

The primary herb is wildewheet.

# Twilight

The twilight grove is associated with the boundaries between day and night, life and death.

The primary herb is moonglow.

# Wild

The wild grove is associated with animal husbandry, breeding, etc.

The primary herb is spirit leaf.
